question — The comment's main move is asking for information, an explanation, or a response, including a direct rhetorical challenge.
reasoned — The comment's main move is a claim supported in the text by a stated cause, concrete example, comparison, observation, or checkable detail.
assertion — The comment's main move is a claim, preference, or reaction without a stated supporting reason or concrete example.

Priority rule: An explicit question or challenge is `question` when responding to it is the main purpose of the comment, even if the author offers context. If a question merely introduces a developed argument, classify the argument as `reasoned`. Between `reasoned` and `assertion`, require support actually written in the comment. An implied reason or confident tone is still `assertion`.
